ORIGINAL PROJECT GUIDE

How to play and what to learn

The Queima-Queima Universe brings together four experiences with different goals. They explore force and accuracy, action and quick screen reading, table tennis strategy, and cryptography and blockchain concepts. This guide explains the rules, presents strategies, and shows how each mechanic relates to learning.

Queima-Queima and Win: force, return, and accuracy

The goal is not simply to launch the piece with as much force as possible. It must reach the final wall and then stop on a scoring zone during its return. A weak launch does not complete the route, while excessive force can waste the positioning opportunity. Scoring comes from balancing power, friction, and reading the board.

Each surface changes the movement. Wood is the reference, glass increases sensitivity, and rubber requires compensation. Repeating the same gesture will not always produce the same strategic result. Locate the highest-value zone, use an initial launch to study the surface, and correct the next attempt according to the difference between the desired and actual positions.

Strategies for improving at Queima-Queima

  • Use early attempts to calibrate the surface instead of immediately seeking maximum force.
  • Compare nearby launches; small corrections are more useful than abrupt changes.
  • In the circuit, adapt whenever the board or scoring zone changes.
  • In duels, consider the accumulated score. A safe move may be better than a risky one.
  • In N-back, focus on the previous position or stimulus indicated by the current level.

N-back adds a working-memory exercise by asking you to compare the current stimulus with one shown a number of steps earlier. It is not a diagnosis or medical treatment; it is a playful attention and memory challenge.

Quebra-Quebra: Crossfire — order, movement, and survival

In Quebra-Quebra, you control a mini cannon and destroy threats in the requested order. Difficulty is not only about speed: you must locate the correct target, move deliberately, and decide when to use upgrades. The campaign includes 100 stages across visual worlds, with progression, bosses, and changes of pace.

Reading the sequence matters as much as aiming. Attacking the wrong target can break the chain and reduce performance. On intense screens, identify the next target first, plan the movement, and only then attack. This short pause reduces impulsive actions and improves consistency.

Modes and campaign preparation

Free play is useful for learning controls and effects. Timed mode trains quick decisions. The campaign gradually combines the elements and records progress. Bonus stages are playable rewards, while bosses reward pattern recognition rather than unplanned repeated attacks.

  • Keep the cannon where it can react to both sides of the screen.
  • Respect the requested order even when another target is closer.
  • Save upgrades for moments when they actually reduce risk.
  • Pause the game when interrupted instead of leaving the campaign running.
  • Use achievements and rankings as progress records, not as an obligation to compete.

JogoBitcoin: cryptography and educational mining

JogoBitcoin represents a simplified blockchain through 100 blocks. To advance, players solve encoding and decoding challenges, record operations, and follow a visual chain. Its purpose is to introduce vocabulary and basic relationships interactively, without performing real mining or offering financial assets.

A Caesar cipher shifts alphabet letters by a defined amount. With key 3, for example, each encrypted letter must be moved three positions in the correct direction. Numbers and some symbols do not belong to the rotation alphabet and should remain unchanged. The visual tool lets players adjust the key and observe the resulting text.

ASCII associates characters with numbers. Base64 represents data using a particular character set and is encoding, not secure encryption. Recognizing the difference matters: encoding changes representation, while encryption aims to protect information with an appropriate key or process.

What the in-game blockchain represents

In a real blockchain, blocks contain records and cryptographic references that help reveal alterations. The game presents that idea visually: every completed challenge adds progress to the chain. This educational mechanic is deliberately simplified; it does not run proof of work, join the Bitcoin network, or generate cryptocurrency.

The wallet, coins, prices, yields, and mining shown in JogoBitcoin are fictional. They support game balance and learning and are not investments, financial recommendations, promises of profit, or redeemable balances. External prices, when available, are informational only.

Using the encyclopedia and tutor

The encyclopedia contains short concepts for consultation during a game. Players can search related terms in external sources, compare explanations, and return to the challenge. The tutor follows the current game context and offers guidance; it can remain active throughout the campaign or be hidden from the menu.

When researching outside the game, check the author, date, and purpose of each source. Technical concepts evolve, and a summary does not replace specialized documentation. External links are starting points for study, not automatic endorsements of everything found there.

Rebate!: positioning, aim, and strategy

In Rebate!, players choose a character and play table tennis against rivals with distinct styles. They must position the paddle, select one of nine destinations, and strike at the right moment. Quick matches and sets teach the controls, while tournaments, Arena, and the World Cup expand the challenge.

In Arena mode, returns charge special moves. Characters have attributes and stories, while challenges, unlockable courts, and a local ranking record progress in this browser. Touch controls on phones provide aiming, spin, and striking actions.

Accessibility, devices, and progress

The games are designed for browsers on computers and phones. Layouts reorganize tools and controls according to available space. On phones, touch controls and menus provide alternative access when a visual shortcut is unavailable.

Preferences, progress, records, and achievements may be kept in browser storage. Clearing site data, changing browsers, or using another device can remove local information. Online features require a connection and may behave differently if hosting is temporarily unavailable.

An evolving project

The four experiences are independently developed and improved through computer and phone testing. Player reports help identify unclear text, touch difficulties, sizing problems, and rules that need better explanations. The contact page can be used to report the device, browser, screen, and exact step where a problem occurred.

The educational content is introductory. JogoBitcoin provides no financial service, sells no cryptocurrency, and does not replace professional technical, legal, or financial advice.
